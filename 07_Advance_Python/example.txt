Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was the 11th President of India and a renowned aerospace scientist.

He is popularly known as the "Missile Man of India" for his contributions to India's missile and space programs.

He played a key role in India's civilian space and defense research through organizations like ISRO and DRDO.

He inspired millions of students with his speeches and books, encouraging them to dream big and work hard.

His famous quote is: "Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
